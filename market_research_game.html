Game Design Overview
Market Research & Customer Discovery

Players take on the role of a startup founder conducting customer discovery interviews, collecting insights, and refining their understanding of the market.

🔄 Core Loop Mechanic

  • Receive a startup scenario brief
  • Choose a customer / stakeholder to interview
  • Navigate a conversation — ask questions, listen, decide
  • Collect insights & earn a quality score
  • Repeat across 3–6 scenarios per level
  • Progress when ≥70% of required data is gathered

📚 Learning Alignment Pedagogy

Every mechanic mirrors a real skill: choosing a stakeholder = stakeholder mapping; dialogue choices = questioning strategy; listening to NPC reactions = active listening; adapting across rounds = iterative thinking. The game loop is the learning process.

🎯 Game Objective Goals

  • Discover customer needs & pain points
  • Fill 70%+ of the insight matrix
  • Build communication & negotiation skills
  • Optional mini-games for bonus points (e.g. arrange best research order)

💬 Feedback System Immediate & Cumulative

  • Immediate: NPC mood shifts, question quality rating
  • Post-meeting: Insight summary, missed follow-ups flagged
  • Cumulative: Skill radar tracking across all sessions
  • Players compare performance over time

🏆 Win Conditions Completion

Win by consistently gathering high-quality insights AND demonstrating good questioning, listening, and negotiation skills. Trust built with NPCs unlocks deeper data. Unsuccessful meetings still count as progress — learning from failure is a deliberate design feature, not a punishment.
