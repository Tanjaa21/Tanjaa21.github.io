How It Works

🔄 Core Loop Mechanic

  • Read your startup brief
  • Pick a stakeholder to interview
  • Navigate 4 dialogue turns — ask, listen, adapt
  • Collect market insights & earn a quality score
  • Complete both scenarios to finish

📚 Learning Alignment Pedagogy

Choosing a stakeholder = stakeholder mapping. Dialogue choices = questioning strategy. NPC mood shifts = active listening signals. The game loop is the learning process.

🎯 Objective Goals

  • Discover customer needs & pain points
  • Build communication & negotiation skills
  • Fill the insight matrix as fully as possible
  • You can advance at any time — no hard blocks

💬 Feedback Immediate & Cumulative

  • Inline: 💭 Reflection after every choice explaining why it worked
  • Post-meeting: Score, insight summary, missed opportunities
  • Skill tracker: Listening, Questioning, Empathy, Adaptability

🏆 Win Conditions Completion

Win by gathering high-quality insights through good questioning. Incomplete or failed meetings still count — learning from failure is intentional, not a punishment.
